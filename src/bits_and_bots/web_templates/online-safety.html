Staying safe online 🛟

Our promises to children

  • Tell a grown-up if anything online makes you feel worried or sad - you'll never be in trouble
  • Keep your full name, school, address and photos private
  • Only use accounts and passwords that your parent or carer set up with you
  • Be kind: treat people online exactly as you would on the playground

For children

  • Think before you share: once it's online, it's out of your hands
  • In games, only chat with people you know in real life
  • If a pop-up says you won something, it's a trick - close it

For parents & carers

  • Agree screen-time and where devices are used (not bedrooms at bedtime)
  • Turn on parental controls on your router and devices
  • Explore together - ask them to teach you what they made in club
  • Report concerns: CEOP Safety Centre

Helpful free resources

  • Thinkuknow (NCA) - age-banded advice and activities
  • Childnet - family agreement templates
  • Internet Watch Foundation - report images
