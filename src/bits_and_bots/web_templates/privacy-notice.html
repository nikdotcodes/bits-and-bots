Our Privacy Policy 🔒

Who we are

Bits and Bots ("we", "us") is the controller of personal data collected through this website. You can contact us at:

  • Email: bitsandbots@nik.codes

What information we collect

Via our "Contact us" form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • Your child's school year (optional)
  • Your message

Via our children's consent/registration form:

  • Your child's first name and initial of surname
  • Your child's year group
  • Parent/guardian name, email address and phone number
  • Emergency contact details
  • Allergy and medical notes relevant to sessions
  • Your confirmation that you have read and agree to our AI use declaration

Why we collect it and our lawful basis

Information Purpose Lawful basis (UK GDPR)
Contact form details To respond to your enquiry Legitimate interests (responding to enquiries) or steps prior to entering a contract
Child's name, year group, parent/guardian details To register your child for sessions and communicate with you Performance of a contract / steps prior to a contract
Emergency contact details To contact someone in an emergency while your child is in our care Legitimate interests (safeguarding); vital interests in an emergency
Allergy and medical notes To keep your child safe during sessions Vital interests (medical care); special category data safeguarded by the Data Protection Act 2018
AI declaration checkbox To record your informed agreement to how AI is used Consent

Note: allergy and medical information is "special category" data. The explicit consent wording you used at the point of collection is:

"I give my explicit consent for Bits and Bots to record and process the allergy and medical information provided above for the sole purpose of keeping my child safe during sessions. I understand this is sensitive personal data, will be shared only with session leaders who need it, and will be deleted at the end of club attendance."

Who we share information with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the website and our activities, each under a data processing agreement:

  • Website hosting provider: Supabase – stores form submissions
  • Email service provider: Protonmail – sends confirmations/communications
  • Any booking or payment system: SumUp - processes payments for our activities

We never use children's personal data to train AI models.

International transfers

All data is stored in the UK.

How long we keep it

  • Contact form enquiries: 12 months after our last reply, then deleted
  • Children's registration records: duration of enrolment plus 12 months
  • Medical/allergy information: deleted at the end of the enrolment period
  • AI declaration records: same period as the registration record

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you or your child
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have data erased in certain circumstances
  • Restrict or object to processing in certain circumstances
  • Data portability where processing is based on consent or contract
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. for the AI declaration or marketing)

To exercise any right, contact us at bitsandbots@nik.codes. We respond within one month.

Children's data: because we collect information about children, we take particular care. Only a parent or guardian may submit a child's information, and we will verify this where appropriate.

Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies for volunteer login sessions.

How we protect your data

Your data is stored in a Supabase database hosted in AWS's London region (eu-west-2), so it remains in the United Kingdom

  • All traffic to and from the database is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Row-level security controls restrict which records each part of the system can access, so data is only readable by the application
  • Access to the database is limited to Nik Walne
  • Form submissions are accessible only to Nik Walne
  • We review access permissions periodically and remove access promptly when someone leaves their role

Supabase acts as our data processor and is contracted under its data processing terms. Although our data resides in the UK, Supabase is based in the United States and may access data to provide support; this is safeguarded under Supabase's Data Processing Addendum (https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/data-processing-addendum).

How to complain

Please contact us first at bitsandbots@nik.codes and we will try to resolve any concern. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, or telephone 0303 123 1113.

AI use declaration

We are committed to using AI responsibly. In summary:

  • We will use AI to develop a classification system for simple data collected from the micro:bit activities (i.e. temparature, gryroscope readings, decibel levels, etc) as part of our learning activities. AI can also be used in the generation of educational content and learning materials, but never to make decisions about children. All AI processing is done on anonymized or aggregated data where possible.
  • We do not enter children's or parents' personal data into public AI tools.
  • The full AI use declaration can be found at {{ BASE_URL }}/safety/ai.
