About our club

Hi, I'm Nik! I'm a local father of 2 and a programmer who believe every child should get to make with technology, not just use it. The club is independent - not attached to any school - and we follow the brilliant free lesson materials from the Micro:bit Educational Foundation.

Our goals

  • Every child leaves able to say "I made this"
  • Confident step from block coding to Python by Year 5/6
  • Healthy habits: teamwork, debugging patience, and staying safe online
  • A warm welcome for children who think coding "isn't for them"

Practical details

  • When: Wednesday's, 3:30-4:30pm, term time
  • Where: Somewhere, Bishops Lydeard
  • Cost: Free! Although donations would be appreciated for more kit that the children can experiment with!

Safeguarding

Every session has a DBS checked lead. Parents sign our consent form before a child's first session, and we publish our online safety promises for children and parents. Questions? Contact us.
