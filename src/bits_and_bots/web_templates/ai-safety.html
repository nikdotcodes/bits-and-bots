AI and us 🤔

Children and parents hear about AI everywhere. Our job is to help children become curious, sceptical and safe around it - and the micro:bit is a great way to learn: the Foundation's Code & AI activities show how machines "learn" from examples.

What children learn in club

  • AI is a tool that makes predictions from data - it can be wrong or unfair
  • We will train a "classifier" themselves, so they know it isn't magic
  • Their own creativity comes first; AI never does the thinking for them

Our club rules for AI tools

  • No AI chatbots used by children during sessions
  • Volunteers may use AI to help prepare materials - always reviewed by a human
  • Children's work, names, photos or writing are never entered into AI tools
  • At home, children use AI tools only with a parent alongside

Talking about AI at home

  • Ask "how do you think it knew that?" - build healthy scepticism
  • Explain that AI answers can sound confident and still be wrong
  • Remind them never to type personal details into any website or app
  • UK guidance: ICO Children's Code (for grown-ups)
